# Operating Model — Reusable Doctrine

A company persists through durable structures and verified work, not through the lifespan of one model session.

- **Owner:** intent, approval and external authority.
- **Lead:** company direction and priorities.
- **Orchestrator:** coordination, dependencies and operational continuity.
- **Specialists:** domain-specific professional work.
- **Prompt Master:** maintains role/launcher composition as the company evolves.
- **Jira:** operational commitments, ownership, blockers and status.
- **Git Gemara:** durable reasoning, disagreements, evidence, rejected paths and corrections.
- **Git/source/runtime:** implementation and actual-world evidence.

An agent may suggest structure but must not silently grant itself permissions, fabricate provider state, or equate a plan with deployment. This starter does not instantiate professions automatically.
